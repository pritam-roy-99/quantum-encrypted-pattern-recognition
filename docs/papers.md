# Uploaded research papers and concept image

This repository is intended to accompany the supplied concept image **Encrypted-Cloning Computation on Quantum Images: How It Works**.

## Paper 1

**Computing on quantum shared secrets**  
Yingkai Ouyang, Si-Hui Tan, Liming Zhao and Joseph F. Fitzsimons. *Physical Review A* **96**, 052333 (2017).  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.96.052333  
Source PDF filename: `PhysRevA.96.052333.pdf`.

This paper establishes a (n,n) quantum secret-sharing scheme supporting Clifford operations on shares, and T gates with the assistance of magic states.

## Paper 2

**Quantum computing on encrypted data**  
K. A. G. Fisher, A. Broadbent, L. K. Shalm, Z. Yan, J. Lavoie, R. Prevedel, T. Jennewein and K. J. Resch. *Nature Communications* **5**, 3074 (2014).  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms4074  
Source PDF filename: `ncomms4074(1).pdf`.

This paper experimentally demonstrates delegated operations on Pauli-encrypted qubits using linear optics.

## Important distinction

Neither reference paper is a demonstration of the proposed encrypted-cloning quantum-image workflow. The Qiskit demo in this repository tests common-Pauli-mask overlap pattern recognition, not the full cloning encoder and recovery maps.
